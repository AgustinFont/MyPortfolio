WHAT I'M LOOKING FOR

I'm a game developer from Argentina, driven by a strong interest in creativity and interactive experiences. I studied videogame development at Da Vinci Escuela Multimedial in Buenos Aires, where I built a solid foundation across design, programming and art.

While studying I worked as a freelancer and, together with two university colleagues, co-founded Nagma — a small independent venture where we learned a lot and worked on large-scale VR, AR and mobile projects.

After graduating I travelled and learned from different cultures, which gave me a broader perspective on how people engage with stories, spaces and challenges. I enjoy bringing that into the experiences I build.

I’m looking for a job that allows me to keep improving as a developer and as a human being. I enjoy projects that challenge my technical thinking and give me space to take ownership of the work I deliver.

I’m confident in what I can bring — focus, adaptability, and a genuine drive to solve problems — and that’s why I look for environments where people take their work seriously but stay open, curious, and willing to help each other.

I work best with a healthy level of flexibility — remote or hybrid — as long as expectations are clear and communication flows well.

If this aligns with what you’re offering, my contact is just one click away.
