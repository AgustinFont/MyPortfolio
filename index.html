ABOUT ME

COVER LETTER

I'm a 25-year old game developer from Argentina, driven by a strong interest in creativity and interactive experiences. I studied videogame development at Da Vinci Escuela Multimedial in Buenos Aires, Argentina, where I built a solid foundation across the different areas of game creation.

During my university years, I received training across the three core disciplines of videogame development: programming, art, and design. Although my main orientation leaned toward designing gameplay and player experiences, working on complete projects required me to actively participate in all areas. Over time, my strongest focus became shaping gameplay and interactive systems, followed closely by C# programming and work with game engines such as Unity and Unreal Engine. I also gained experience creating assets and materials using tools like Substance Painter, Substance Designer, Quixel, and 3ds Max, as well as a foundation in graphic design and animation using Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, and After Effects.

While studying, I worked as a freelancer on different projects. Together with two university friends, I co-founded Nagma, a small independent venture where we learned a lot and had the opportunity to work on large-scale projects, including VR, AR, and mobile applications.

After graduating, I had the opportunity to travel and learn from different cultures, which gave me a broader perspective on how people engage with stories, spaces, and challenges. I enjoy bringing that perspective into the interactive experiences I build.
